# 💰 Expense Tracker

A simple, sleek, and efficient 💸 **Expense Tracker Web App** to help you monitor your spending, control your budget, and visualize your financial habits — all in your browser! 🌐  

---

## ✨ Features

🧾 **Add and Manage Expenses**  
Easily record your daily expenses with description, category, and amount.  

📅 **Automatic Date Tracking**  
Each expense is logged with the current date to make history tracking easy.  

💾 **Persistent Data Storage**  
Your data is stored in the browser’s **local storage**, so even after refreshing — your expenses stay safe!  

📊 **Dynamic Expense Display**  
All expenses appear instantly in a clear, responsive list format.  

🎨 **Attractive UI**  
Built with clean HTML, stylish CSS, and simple animations to keep things smooth and intuitive.
